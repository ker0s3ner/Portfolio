FaceLab

FaceLab is a local computer-vision playground for exploring face detection, landmarks, and visual feedback without turning a personal image into a remote data product.

What it demonstrates

  • Camera and image-input experiments.
  • Detection and landmark visualization.
  • Small, inspectable TypeScript prototypes.

Privacy boundary

Processing should remain local by default. Any future hosted demo must make camera access explicit and avoid retaining uploaded frames.
