\subsection{Research & Proofs}

The Spectral Theorem: Foundations, Applications, Modern Perspective

mathematical monograph • functional analysis

An 11-page mathematical monograph that builds a compact route to the spectral theorem. It begins with inner-product spaces, induced norms, orthogonality, projections, completeness, and Hilbert spaces, then develops orthonormal bases and Fourier series before introducing adjoints, self-adjoint operators, eigenvalue decompositions, and spectral theory in finite and infinite dimensions. The manuscript closes with the projection-valued-measure form of the theorem, connecting the elementary and operator-theoretic viewpoints.

functional analysis Hilbert spaces spectral theory operator theory Fourier analysis

On the Differential Geometry of Ideal Rocket Trajectories

original research • differential geometry • optimal control

A foundational differential-geometric treatment of ideal rocket trajectories under vacuum operation. Constructed the rocket phase manifold $(M,g)$, a Riemannian 2-manifold where the Tsiolkovsky delta-v relation emerges as a geodesic arc-length formula. Proved eight main theorems including: the Arc-Length Theorem identifying $\Delta v$ with $L_g(\gamma)$, the Geodesic Theorem characterizing constant-thrust burns as unique geodesics, the Flatness Theorem proving $R \equiv 0$ and deriving global isometry with Euclidean $\mathbb{R}^2$, and the Conjugate-Point Theorem establishing global optimality. Extended results to Hamiltonian mechanics on the cotangent bundle, symplectic reduction, and multi-stage rocket optimization via fiber-bundle splicing. Complete with rigorous proofs and numerical validation across multiple propellant systems.

differential geometry Riemannian manifolds optimal control aerospace Hamiltonian mechanics

Determinant Congruence for Sum-Entry Matrices

linear algebra

Proved that for any $n \times n$ matrix $A$ with entries $A_{ij} = i + j$, the determinant satisfies $\det(A) \equiv 0 \pmod{n}$ for all $n \geq 3$, and in fact $\det(A) = 0$ exactly. The proof demonstrates that all columns of $A$ lie in the span of two vectors $u = (1,1,\ldots,1)^T$ and $v = (1,2,\ldots,n)^T$, establishing linear dependence when $n \geq 3$. Verified the result through row operations on small cases and generalized using vector space dimension arguments. Includes explicit computations for $n=1,2$ edge cases where the determinant is non-zero.

Bastille

systems security • privacy-hardened Linux

A privacy-hardened desktop Linux live system, a spiritual successor to the 2002 Shmoo Group Tinfoil Hat Linux, rebuilt for modern threat models. Boots from USB, runs in RAM, and leaves nothing on the host machine. Features a WireGuard kill-switch with nftables, LUKS2 Argon2id split-key encryption, a three-PIN duress system (normal / decoy / full-wipe), USB forensic tool detection (Cellebrite, MSAB, Qualcomm EDL), BadUSB blocking, Intel ME stripping via me_cleaner, OpenCV face detection for automatic screen lock, i2pd I2P routing inside the WireGuard tunnel, and key material secured with sodium_malloc + mlock. Sway/Wayland compositor; Debian base.

Python C linux cryptography privacy WireGuard LUKS2

Holdshort

simulation • aviation • systems programming

A ground-operations and terminal-airspace ATC simulator built with Tauri (Rust core + React/TypeScript frontend). Uses real-world taxiway, runway, and gate geometry pulled from OpenStreetMap with A* taxi routing and a full ramp/ground/tower clearance state machine. Three displays: Surface Movement Radar, a Tower Cab view over satellite imagery, and an Approach radar scope with range rings and datablocks. Approach arrivals are vectored in from a spawn point on final rather than teleported. Every unworked position runs on its own AI timer so a single-position session still produces complete moving traffic. Ships with 12 bundled airports including KJFK, KATL, EGLL, RJTT, and EDDF.
