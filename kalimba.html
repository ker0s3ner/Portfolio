Kalimba

Kalimba is a Kotlin Minecraft utility client focused on a coherent, configurable experience: a fast module workflow, readable HUD components, and a custom visual language instead of a collection of copied screens.

Current direction

The client combines the responsiveness and modularity associated with modern PvP clients with a calmer, more intentional theme system. The companion theme work lives in src-theme/.

Engineering focus

  • Predictable module lifecycle and configuration.
  • Own start menu, click GUI, HUD, notifications, and settings surfaces.
  • Accessible contrast and localized UI strings.
